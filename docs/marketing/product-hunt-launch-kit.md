## First maker comment

Hey Product Hunt 👋

I'm a student building PLAYFORGE.

I wanted a more engaging way to work on everyday goals without turning self-improvement into another all-or-nothing system. So I started building a game-inspired app where routines become quests and progress is something you can actually see.

With PLAYFORGE, you can:
- Turn everyday routines into daily quests.
- Track workouts and movement, with exercise guidance built into sessions.
- Log meals and hydration, and review your activity over time.
- Build a player profile and keep your progress synced across devices.

I also made a free seven-day challenge for anyone who wants to try one small action at a time: https://playforge-fawn.vercel.app/challenge/

PLAYFORGE is still evolving, and I'm here to learn from real users. I'd especially appreciate feedback on:
1. Does the quest system make it easier to start a routine?
2. Which part feels confusing or unnecessary?
3. What would make you come back next week?

Thanks for taking a look. Honest feedback is much more useful than a polite upvote.

## Gallery plan — use real app captures, not mockups

Product Hunt's current editor requires a logo, at least one gallery image, launch tags, and maker/hunter role. Three or more images and a demo/video are recommended extras.

Capture these from the working production app after a manual smoke test:

1. **Daily quests:** Actual Quests screen with representative quest cards. Hide personal email, profile names, and private data.
2. **Workout session:** Actual workout with the form-coaching panel visible. Do not imply form is automatically verified.
3. **Progress:** Actual Progress screen with non-identifying sample/demo data. Do not invent user counts, retention, or measured outcomes.

Use the existing product mark at \`/playforge.svg\` for the square logo. Use genuine screenshots exported at 1270×760 or a similar wide ratio for the gallery. Keep text readable and avoid claims the live app cannot demonstrate.

## Launch-day approach

- Submit from the personal maker account. You can hunt your own product.
- Ask people to visit, try the app, and leave honest comments if they have feedback. Never ask directly for upvotes or offer rewards for votes.
- Be present to answer questions and acknowledge limitations.
- Do not buy votes, use engagement pods, or mass-message strangers.
- Track real activation and returning users, not leaderboard position.
