THE SEVEN-DAY RUN

Your next 7 small wins.

Seven simple actions. No pressure.
Miss a day? Pick up whenever you’re ready.

Your challenge progress0 of 7 complete
DAY01
Choose your focus◎

Pick one thing that matters.

Choose a routine or personal goal you want to make easier. Write down the smallest version you could realistically do today.

Try: read two pages, tidy one corner, or take a short walk.

DAY02
Make it repeatable◷

Give your goal a tiny time slot.

Attach your action to something already in your day: after breakfast, before a study session, or when you get home.

Keep it small enough to repeat.

DAY03
Move in a way that fits↗

Make room for movement.

Try a comfortable walk, stretch, sport, or workout that suits your ability and energy. Rest is also a valid choice.

Stop if something feels painful or wrong.

DAY04
Reset your environment✦

Make the next good choice easier.

Prepare one thing that supports your routine: keep your book visible, set out comfortable clothes, or clear your workspace.

A helpful setup beats relying on motivation.

DAY05
Repeat, don't escalate⟳

Do your small action again.

Repeat the version that felt manageable. You do not need to add more time, more reps, or more tasks to make progress count.

Consistency can start very small.

DAY06
Notice what helps▥

Spot one thing that made it easier.

Think about your energy, timing, environment, or the way you broke the task down. Keep the one change that helped most.

Adapt the plan to real life.

DAY07
Reflect and choose⚑

Keep one thing for next week.

What felt useful? What felt unrealistic? Choose one small action to carry forward, and let the rest go.

Progress is information, not a test.
